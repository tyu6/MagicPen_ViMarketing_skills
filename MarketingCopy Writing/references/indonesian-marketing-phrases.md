# Indonesian Furniture Marketing Phrase Bank (2026 Edition)

## Preface

This phrase bank contains marketing expressions commonly used by successful Indonesian furniture sellers on e-commerce platforms and social media. These phrases are designed to sound natural and appealing to Indonesian consumers.

**Note**: This is an expandable reference. Add new phrases as market trends evolve.

## 10. Natural vs. Formal Expression Guide

| Context | Natural (Recommended) | Formal (Avoid) |
|---------|-----------------------|----------------|
| Quality | Kualitas juara! | Produk ini memiliki kualitas yang sangat baik |
| Durability | Awet banget lho! | Produk ini memiliki daya tahan yang lama |
| Comfort | Emang nyaman, ga akan nyesel! | Produk ini memberikan kenyamanan optimal |
| Price | Harga terjangkau, ga bikin kantong jebol | Harga produk ini sangat kompetitif |
| CTA | Buruan diorder, stok terbatas! | Silakan melakukan pembelian segera |
